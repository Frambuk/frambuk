Important: Pixels do not have a fixed physical size.

A pixel is a digital measurement. Its physical size depends on the pixel density (DPI/PPI). For example, at 96 DPI, 96 pixels correspond to 1 inch. At 300 DPI, 300 pixels correspond to 1 inch. Change the DPI above to calculate different physical-size equivalents.
