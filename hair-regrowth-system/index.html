Your first 45 days, simplified

  1. Release tension each morning. A short massage loosens the scalp before you apply your activator.
  2. Feed your follicles daily. Protein-rich, low-sugar meals, water and consistent routines.
  3. Go deep at night. Night oil plus a full massage session while your scalp repairs.
  4. Track it properly. Photos on days 1, 14, 30 and 40, plus the free tracker so you see slow progress.
  5. Stay through the shedding phase. Know what is normal in weeks 2 to 8 so you do not quit early.
